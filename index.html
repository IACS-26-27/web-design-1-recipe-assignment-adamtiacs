The following steps to Survive Freshman Year

The first step is to have a balance of classes that you know that you would do well on and pick a couple of challenging classes

The next step is to find and make friends in your classes. Some of which could be ones that you hang out with

Find something that interests you

It could be anything new that you think you might like such as ...

  • Join a club, organization, or volunteer
  • Coding or journalism
  • Playing a sport
  • Art or music
